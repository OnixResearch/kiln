## Context

The 23 navigation destinations and the project detail route serve different operator tasks. Their exact URLs and ownership boundaries are already established.

## Decisions

### Decision: Simplify hierarchy, not capabilities

**Choice:** Keep routine destinations in the rail and group secondary tools by existing section. Lead pages with the user's task and separate routine Fleet operations from supporting observations.

**Rationale:** Clear priority costs fewer decisions without changing routes, effects, or evidence provenance.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- Do not hide warnings, action scope, or the only path to any destination. Deep links must open their containing navigation disclosure.
