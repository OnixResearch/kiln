## Context

The rail presents two primary sections and three separate disclosures before the separate Guide and pin controls.

## Decisions

### Decision: One secondary disclosure

**Choice:** Keep one flat primary list and organize the remaining links under a native More tools disclosure. Automatically reveal the current secondary destination when entering it, and collapse the disclosure when returning to a primary route.

**Rationale:** Shorten the default rail without inventing routes or removing destinations. The numbered register, command palette, Guide, desktop pin and mobile drawer remain intact.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- Secondary routes require an extra action in the rail. Keep them directly addressable by URL and discoverable through command search.
