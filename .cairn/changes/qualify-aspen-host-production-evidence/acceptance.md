# Change-local acceptance

a[qualify-aspen-host-production-evidence.cohort] Qualification MUST bind the actual source, executable, runtime/profile, provider and publication identities. Historical results MUST retain their original cohort and scope; only matching observations may be reused.

a[qualify-aspen-host-production-evidence.execution] Isolated native execution MUST cover real-process durability/recovery, corruption, owned provider teardown, finite named load, complete quiescent backup/restore, explicit rollback and authority boundaries. Positive and negative results MUST retain actual commands, bounds, outputs and artifact identities without disrupting deployed services.

a[qualify-aspen-host-production-evidence.admission] Complete passing exact-cohort evidence MAY support only the bounded local host behavior observed. Qualification MUST reject missing, failed, stale or mixed evidence and promotion into global durability, exactly-once delivery, CI correctness, provider truth, sandboxing, availability, or implicit release/deployment authority.

a[qualify-aspen-host-production-evidence.gates] Required focused and broad gates MUST pass for the recorded cohort before qualification is reported. Failures MUST be retained, accepted requirement bodies and policy MUST remain unchanged, and traceability MUST link real implementation and executable verification rather than merely restating IDs.
