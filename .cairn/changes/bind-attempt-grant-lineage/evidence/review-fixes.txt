Review fixes on top of 4d95d16f (independent review history://KilnS2Review: 2 P2, 4 P3).

What landed:

1. [P2] `AttemptLedger::bind_authority(identity, binding, now_ms)`: after the
   lineage, rebind and inactive checks, a first binding now calls
   `require_active(identity, binding.fence, now_ms)`. An identical binding still
   returns early. Negatives: lease expired (`implementation_lease_expired`) and
   A1 still `Claimed` after A2 replaced it, bound at a clock inside A1's old
   lease (`implementation_fence_stale`).
2. [P2] `AttemptLineage.project` (from `ImplementationWork.project`).
   `admit_root` refuses `foreign_lineage` when the ceiling's project differs,
   including a project-wide ceiling; `check_current` compares the project on
   every link. Negatives: root ceiling for another project, a project-wide
   ceiling from another project (and a positive project-wide ceiling for the
   work's own project), and an attempt in another project under a child.
3. [P3] Lineage refusal order: campaign revision, project, work and version
   (foreign), then fence (stale), then attempt identity (foreign). New
   fixtures: successor attempt with a new fence (root and child) =>
   stale_generation; other work with a new fence => foreign_lineage. The
   predecessor-fence fixtures still give stale_generation.
4. [P3] `check_current`'s parent branch also requires exactly one role that
   the parent holds (`role_derives`) and the shared-or-disjoint budget rule
   (`budget_derives`); `admit_child` uses the same two helpers.
5. [P3] `check_current_refuses_every_recomputing_link_that_breaks_lineage`:
   resealed links that recompute (wider lifetime, wider route, depth 2 directly
   under the root, other root policy, mixed roles, unrelated budget) each give
   foreign_lineage. The dimension sweep now also runs under a project-wide
   parent and asserts admission against an explicit oracle independent of
   `covers` (12 and 24 admitted children).
6. [P3] `GrantIssuanceState::reconcile(now_s, authority_generation, attempt)`
   returns `Issue` only for an unissued intent with the current generation and
   exact attempt lineage (`AdmittedGrant::binds`), otherwise `Abandon`.
   Negatives: moved generation, replaced attempt and fence, another project.

Notes: the `AdmittedGrant` doc and design now say recovery resumes the stored
intent; design records that S6 owns blocking a second root and alternative
proofs and comparing the incarnation with the recorded launch, and that the
shell never builds current facts for a non-current attempt. The delta spec's
root, foreign, identity, attempt and issuance requirements were updated to match.
