## Decisions

The request is operator-supplied because the implementation workflow belongs to
Lattice. Kiln binds it to its reservation and attempt before transport. A
disconnect after acceptance is unknown, not success or failure; the accepted
execution identity can be reconciled. The graph is restricted to agent/local/
custom nodes to avoid widening the review or CI route.

## Non-claims

No Lattice workflow is authored, no worker is selected, and transport acceptance
is not a candidate, verification, integration or completion result.
