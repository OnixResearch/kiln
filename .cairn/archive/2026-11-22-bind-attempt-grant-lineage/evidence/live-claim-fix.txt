Live-claim fix on top of c71fc553.

What landed:

- [P3] `GrantIssuanceState::reconcile(now_ms, authority_generation, attempts,
  current)` takes the `AttemptLedger` and returns `Issue` only when the
  resolver gives `Some`, `binds` holds, and
  `attempts.is_current(&grant.attempt, grant.fence, now_ms)` holds (plus the
  existing generation, revoked-ancestor and expiry rules); otherwise
  `Abandon`. `now_ms` replaces the separate `now_s` so one host clock drives
  both the lease and the grant lifetime (`now_ms / MS_PER_SECOND`).
- Tests now claim real attempts in an `AttemptLedger`. New test
  `a_lapsed_lease_abandons_before_expire_runs`: an attempt whose lease lapsed
  but which `expire` has not processed (still active, still the resolver's
  current attempt) gives `Abandon`, beside a live attempt that gives `Issue`.
- Doc comment, design and the spec's issuance requirement and scenario
  updated; design notes that a `FinishActive` predecessor is still the live
  claim and may issue by design, while `authorize_publication` refuses
  superseded attempts.

Mutation check: replacing `!attempts.is_current(..)` with a condition that is
always false made `a_lapsed_lease_abandons_before_expire_runs` FAIL; the line
was restored (the file's snapshot hash returned to its pre-mutation value).
