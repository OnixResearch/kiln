## Why

The live CI workspace lacks collaborative work capture, planning, personal attention and durable views. The operator requested all previously compared Linear-style workflows through native COBs, creating new COB types where needed.

## What Changes

Add a separately admitted, signed local COB work workspace: issues, planning entities, discussion, triage/customer requests, personal attention/subscriptions/reminders, saved views/favorites/boards, keyboard command/search/preview/multiselect and a source/review/CI/evidence-linked change detail.

## Impact

Current consumer: Kiln internal developers and self-CI operators. Kiln dashboard/service maintainers own the application composition; native Git/Radicle storage remains behind its existing pinned port. CI authority stays in Kiln and Cairn lifecycle authority stays in Cairn. Durable capability is replayable native work history and repeatable desktop/mobile workflows, not a one-time imported dataset.

## Out of Scope

Hosted billing, external CRM deployment, automatic publication/synchronization, production-store mutation, existing operator key use without separate admission, lifecycle acceptance and replacing Radicle/Cairn semantics.
