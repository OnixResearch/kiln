## Decisions

The acceptance digest is derived from the work identity and sorted obligations,
not supplied by the worker. A result for another work version or obligation set
therefore cannot be transferred by relabeling the outer envelope.

Changed paths are captured by the provider shell from the actual workspace; this
contract checks their shape and scope but cannot prove completeness. Evidence
references are retained separately with an explicit outcome, but admission does
not promote any outcome into verification.

The handoff is stored on the attempt beside the candidate and phase, so restart
preserves the exact envelope. Repeating an equal handoff returns the retained
digest; a different one is a conflict.

## Non-claims

No check is executed, no run or artifact is fetched, and no candidate is
accepted. A passed evidence observation is not trusted verification. Cairn

authoritative completion and the integration queue remain separate consumers.
