# Fleet operator control plane

## Why

The Fleet and Cairn registers present separate observations. Operators need to inspect exact work, see why it is stuck, and reach existing guarded controls without reconstructing identity or authority from labels.

## What Changes

- Connect exact attempt inspection, Fleet attention and recovery, queue and capacity evidence, runtime attribution and campaign controls.
- Preserve provider/owner authority, observation freshness and unsupported operations.

## Impact

- Fleet and Cairn console, owner projections and guarded commands.
- Maintenance: Kiln web and Fleet maintainers; provider-native evidence remains owned by its producer.
